Jalebi

Jalebi is a popular, spiral-shaped, deep-fried sweet made from flour and soaked in sugar syrup

Modak

it is a sweet dumpling featuring a soft outer shell of rice flour and a sweet filling of grated coconut and jaggery

Soan Papdi

soan papdi is a traditional sweet with a light flaky and melt-in-the mouth texture

Ladoo

it is a round sweet made from flour ,ghee and sugar or jaggery

Kaju Katli

it is a sweet dish made from smooth cashew paste and sugar

Rasmalai

it is a classic South Asian dessert made of soft, spongy cottage cheese discs soaked in sweetened, thickened, cardamom- and saffron-infused milk
